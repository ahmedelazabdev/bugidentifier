About Our Scientific Diagnostic Platform

Free Bug Identifier was developed to address a critical domestic health and environmental issue: insect misidentification that triggers excessive and dangerous household chemical pesticide use. Every month, thousands of harmless carpet beetles, cellar spiders, and wood borers are sprayed with toxic synthetic sprays simply because they were mistaken for bed bugs, brown recluses, or carpenter ants.

Our Methodology & Taxonomic Integrity

Our visual diagnostics evaluate key morphological markers standardized by the Entomological Society of America (ESA):

  • Appendage Count & Segmentation: Differentiating 6-legged hexapods from 8-legged arachnids and multi-legged myriapods.
  • Wing Morphology: Detecting hardened elytra (beetles), membranous hemelytra (true bugs), or flightless morphology (bed bugs).
  • Pronotum & Cephalothorax Markings: Detecting parallel bars on German cockroaches and violin indentations on recluse species.
