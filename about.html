Our Mission & Scientific Methodology

EntomoScan was established to solve a critical public health and environmental crisis: insect misidentification leading to indiscriminate chemical pesticide usage. Each year, millions of harmless spiders and vital pollinating beetles are eradicated in residential households because they are mistaken for bed bugs, ticks, or venomous arachnids.

Taxonomic Dichotomous Key

Unlike purely automated black-box machine learning APIs, EntomoScan couples client-side feature detection with dichotomous morphological keys standard in professional academic entomology. Our algorithms evaluate diagnostic traits including:

  • Antennal morphology: Filiform, clavate, serrate, or geniculate arrangements.
  • Wing vein architecture: Presence of elytra, hemelytra, or halter balancers.
  • Tarsal segmentation: Leg joint configurations that conclusively separate beetle superfamilies from true bugs.
