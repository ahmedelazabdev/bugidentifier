Privacy Policy & Photo Security

Effective Date: March 2026

At EntomoScan, we treat user privacy with absolute seriousness. Unlike commercial identification applications that upload your domestic interior photos to remote cloud servers for behavioral modeling and advertising profiling, EntomoScan guarantees zero cloud transmission of private photos.

1. Client-Side Image Execution

When you select or drop a photo into our scanner, the file is parsed exclusively in your local browser sandbox through HTML5 FileAPI and Canvas elements. At no point is the photo transferred to an external database, logged, or indexed by our servers.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect or read EXIF metadata (such as GPS coordinates or camera serial numbers) from your images.
  • We do not require user accounts, emails, telephone numbers, or login credentials to access the full capabilities of our tools.
  • We do not sell user interaction profiles to pest extermination syndicates or advertising exchanges.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We do not use tracking or cross-site tracking cookies. We only use local session storage to retain your diagnostic filter selections during active usage, which resets once your browser session is terminated.

4. Entomological & Medical Disclaimer

The diagnostic guidance provided by EntomoScan is intended for educational, taxonomical, and non-emergency identification purposes only. If you have been stung or bitten and experience symptoms of anaphylaxis, extreme localized swelling, or severe pain, seek immediate emergency medical care. EntomoScan does not replace clinical physician evaluations.
