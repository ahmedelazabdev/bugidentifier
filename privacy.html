Privacy Policy & On-Device Security

Effective Date: October 2026

Your privacy is fundamentally respected on Free Bug Identifier. Many commercial insect scanning applications silently upload personal household photos to remote servers for machine learning harvesting and ad retargeting. We operate on a strict zero cloud upload principle.

1. Purely Local Client-Side Execution

When you select or drop a photo into our scanner, the image is read strictly within your browser’s local sandboxed memory via HTML5 Canvas and File API objects. At no point are your pictures transmitted over the network or saved to remote databases.

2. Data We Never Collect

  • No photo uploads, camera telemetry, or EXIF metadata extraction.
  • No user account registration, passwords, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No sharing or selling of residential search data to exterminator marketing networks.

3. Medical & Safety Disclaimer

The diagnostic recommendations provided by this tool are intended strictly for educational, morphological, and domestic reference purposes. If you or a family member have experienced a painful bite, stinging reaction, or exhibit signs of severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis), seek immediate clinical medical attention.
